About Us

Crown Beauty start as a small business in Nelspruit, South Africa, in 2020.
Founded by Penny and eventually grew to be one of the leading hair product suppliers in the country.
Our mission is to provide our customers with the highest quality hair products at affordable prices.
We are committed to providing excellent customer service and ensuring that our customers are satisfied with their purchases.

We believe that everyone deserves to feel confident and beautiful, and we strive to help our customers achieve that through our products.
At Crown Beauty, we are passionate about hair and beauty, and we are dedicated to helping our customers look and feel their best.
We are constantly updating our product offerings to ensure that we are providing the latest and greatest in hair products.
Thank you for choosing Crown Beauty as your go-to destination for all things hair.
We look forward to serving you and helping you achieve your hair goals.
